# Credits

## Engine

- **Moth Quantum Atlas, telablur-v1 (Quantum Teleblur).** Every frame of this piece is a completed
  telablur-v1 job on Atlas's classical statevector simulator. Job IDs are in [PARAMS.md](PARAMS.md).

## Papers (verified via PubMed; DOIs below)

- **Y. Cao, V. Fatemi, S. Fang, K. Watanabe, T. Taniguchi, E. Kaxiras, P. Jarillo-Herrero**,
  "Unconventional superconductivity in magic-angle graphene superlattices", *Nature* **556**, 43–50 (2018).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/nature26160 . Source of "about 1.1°, the first magic angle".
- **R. Bistritzer, A. H. MacDonald**, "Moiré bands in twisted double-layer graphene",
  *PNAS* **108**, 12233–12237 (2011). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1108174108 . The flat-band prediction.
- The moiré period L = a / (2 sin(θ/2)) and graphene's lattice constant a = 0.246 nm are standard
  geometry and textbook values, not results of this piece.

## Data

No third-party data. Every image was generated by the code in this folder: `lattice.py` draws the
honeycomb layers (synthetic), telablur-v1 produced the morphs, and `measure.py` / `make_video.py` /
`build_web.py` made the maps, plot, video and page.

## Characters

The pixel-art cast (Ay and Bee the honeycomb layers, Lec the electron, Echo the register ghost, the rim grip
and small props) was drawn for this piece in code by `make_sprites.py` (`web/sprites.json`), in the shared
ink palette. They are drawn on the page by the project's shared helper `common/inksprite.js`, and Lec is
exported as the hub mascot `web/img/mascot.png` by `common/mascot.py`. No third-party art.
