# Credits

## Data

**No real scroll data is used.** The scroll, its papyrus texture, CT-like artefacts, carbon flecks, stroke debris and
ink are all generated by `gen_scroll.py` (seeded, reproducible). Scan data from the Vesuvius Challenge and other
scroll projects is under restrictive licences, so none of it was downloaded or consulted for this piece.

## Text

- The hidden line is the Epicurean *tetrapharmakos* ("four-part cure"), as preserved by Philodemus in the Herculaneum
  papyrus PHerc. 1005 (col. 5, lines 9–14, per the Wikipedia article below). An ancient text, in the public domain.
  We write it in capitals with word spaces and middle dots; the English translation on the page is ours.
  Checked against https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrapharmakos
- Letters rasterised with Palatino Linotype Bold (a Windows system font) into our own image only; no font file is
  redistributed.

## Engine and libraries

- **Moth Quantum Atlas, blur-v1 (Quantum Blur) v1.1.9.** Built on the QuantumBlur library
  (https://github.com/qiskit-community/QuantumBlur).
- Python: numpy, scipy, pillow. Page: plain JavaScript and WebGL 1, no external scripts.
- Pixel art (the archaeologist moth, its moods, the desk props, bubbles and sparkles): original, drawn for this piece in
  the shared ink palette; stored in `web/sprites.json` and drawn with the shared helper `common/inksprite.js`. The hub
  mascot `web/img/mascot.png` is rendered from the same pixels by `common/mascot.py`.
- Brand kit and fonts: `common/brand.css` (Geist and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts), the paper-and-ultramarine look of the Moth Hack 2026 brief pages. No Moth logo or wordmark is used.
