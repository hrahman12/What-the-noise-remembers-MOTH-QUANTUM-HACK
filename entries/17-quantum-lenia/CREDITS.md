# Credits

## Science

- **Bert Wang-Chak Chan**, "Lenia: Biology of Artificial Life", *Complex Systems* 28(3), 251–286 (2019),
  doi:10.25088/ComplexSystems.28.3.251, arXiv:1812.05433. This is the source of the Lenia rule, Orbium's
  settings (R 13, T 10, μ 0.15, σ 0.015) and the exponential kernel core. The citation was verified against
  the arXiv record.

## Data

- **Orbium unicaudatus pattern** (`orbium.py`, `orbium.npy`): the `"O2u"` entry of `Python/animals.json` in
  Bert Chan's Lenia repository, https://github.com/Chakazul/Lenia, decoded from Lenia's RLE format.
  **MIT License, Copyright (c) 2018 Bert Chan.** We checked the licence at
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Chakazul/Lenia/master/LICENSE.md, and the MIT notice applies to this
  pattern. We checked by simulation that it glides under `lenia.py` (mass steady at about 71 over 3,000 steps).
- Everything else (kernel shells, worlds, snapshots, film frames, and the pixel-art sprites in `web/sprites.json`,
  drawn by `make_sprites.py` and rendered with the shared `common/inksprite.js`) was generated by the code in
  this project.
  No third-party image data is used.

## Engine

- **Moth Quantum Atlas, blur-core-v1** (Quantum Blur Core): Gray-coded amplitude encoding with per-qubit
  Rx rotations, in the QuantumBlur family (https://github.com/qiskit-community/QuantumBlur).

## Software and fonts

- numpy, scipy, Pillow, requests, imageio-ffmpeg (FFmpeg, H.264 via libx264), Node 24 (tests), Google
  Chrome headless (layout check only).
- Page fonts: Geist and IBM Plex Mono via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License), from the shared common/brand.css.
- Film captions are rendered with the Windows system fonts Segoe UI (Light and Regular) and Consolas (`render_mp4.py`),
  standing in for the page's Geist and IBM Plex Mono.
- No extra pip packages were installed for this piece.
