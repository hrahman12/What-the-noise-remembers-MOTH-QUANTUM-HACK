# Credits

## Data

No third-party data files are used. The vibrational "chords" in `spectra.py` are approximate,
rounded textbook infrared group frequencies (standard correlation-table values for C-H stretches,
C=O stretches, CH2/CH3 bends, aromatic ring modes). They are general facts, typed in by hand, and are
not copied from any particular spectrum or database. The deuterated lines are our own harmonic
estimates (C-H frequencies divided by sqrt(mu_CD / mu_CH) = 1.362), computed in `spectra.py`.
All MIDI, WAV and image files in this folder were generated by the code here or downloaded from our
own Atlas jobs.

## Odour descriptions (shown under the molecule's name)

- Acetophenone: "sweet pungent" odour of orange blossom, almond-like. PubChem compound summary, CID 7410,
  https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7410
- Cyclopentadecanone (Exaltone): musk, sweet, powdery, animalic. The Good Scents Company,
  https://www.thegoodscentscompany.com/data/rw1025311.html
- Muscone: the main odorant of deer musk. Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscone and the ACS
  Molecule of the Week, https://www.acs.org/molecule-of-the-week/archive/m/muscone.html
- The notes shown for the deuterated forms restate results of the papers above (Turin 1996; Keller &
  Vosshall 2004; Gane et al. 2013; Block et al. 2015). Molar masses are computed from the formulas with
  standard atomic weights (C 12.011, H 1.008, D 2.014, O 15.999).

## Illustrations

Plate I (sagittal section of the nose), Plate II (olfactory epithelium, odorant receptor, tunnelling
diagram) and every pixel sprite are original drawings made for this page, schematic and not to scale, not
traced from any source. Anatomical names and their Latin forms follow Terminologia Anatomica (TA98, FIPAT;
e.g. *ostium pharyngeum tubae auditivae* in *pars nasalis pharyngis*,
https://ifaa.unifr.ch/Public/EntryPage/TA98%20Tree/Latin%20TA98/05.3.01.011%20Latin%20TA98.htm), and the
extent of the olfactory region (*pars olfactoria tunicae mucosae nasi*) was checked against IMAIOS e-Anatomy
(https://www.imaios.com/en/e-anatomy/anatomical-structures/olfactory-part-of-nasal-mucosa-1541214008).
Molecule sprites are drawn from the standard structures of acetophenone, cyclopentadecanone and muscone.
The mascot (a head in midline section sniffing acetophenone) is generated procedurally by `make_sprites.py`
from simple shapes; the sniff sound on the page is synthesised in the browser from filtered noise.

## Engines and libraries

- **Moth Quantum Atlas, `blur-midi-v1`** ("Blur Jazz": a unitary quantum blur for MIDI piano rolls), run
  on Atlas's statevector simulator.
- **mido** (MIDI read/write), **NumPy** (synthesis), **Matplotlib** (`hero.png`), **imageio-ffmpeg**
  (the bundled ffmpeg encodes the page's mp3s: the tour and the 60 chord WAVs), Python 3.12.
- Page: Web Audio API (sine synthesis in the browser), Canvas 2D, Google Fonts (Geist, IBM Plex Mono),
  the shared brand kit `common/brand.css` and the shared sprite helper `common/inksprite.js` (both inlined). No external scripts.
