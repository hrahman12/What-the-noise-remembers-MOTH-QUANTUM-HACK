## Data

No third-party data is used. The three Ising problems (a 20-node ring, a 2 x 10 ladder, and a random 3-regular spin
glass with fixed seeds) are generated by `problems.py`. Every bitstring on the page is either a THRML sample or a
graph-v1 shot.

## Art and sound

All the art is original and made for this piece. The large drawings of the two fighters (the stochastic magnetic tunnel
junction and the dilution refrigerator), the boss's chip and the magnet board are SVG ink engravings written in code in
`web/template.html`. The button icons and the hub mascot (`web/img/mascot.png`, the junction beside the fridge) are
pixel art drawn in code by `make_sprites.py`, in the shared ink palette, and rendered with the shared
`common/inksprite.js` helper and `common/mascot.py`. The sound effects (the magnets' clicks, the sizzle, the chime, the
hit, the countdown, the boss's three pulse-tube "chirps") are synthesised in the browser with Web Audio: there are no
audio files and no third-party samples.
