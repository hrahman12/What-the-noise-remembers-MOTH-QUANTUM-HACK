# Credits

## Data

- **Tartan Ribbon (1861)**, three-colour photograph by James Clerk Maxwell (photographs by Thomas Sutton).
  Wikimedia Commons, `File:Tartan Ribbon.jpg`, 1100×900 JPEG, SHA-1 `a6e7b49bf2f467a9dfb337a1d121832e0f86e430`.
  - File page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tartan_Ribbon.jpg
  - Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7f/Tartan_Ribbon.jpg
  - **Licence: public domain.** Verified on the file page on 5 Oct 2026 (UTC). Its licensing section says
    the author died in 1879, so the work is public domain where copyright lasts life + 100 years or fewer,
    and in the US because it was published before 1 January 1931 (categories `PD-old-100-expired`, `CC-PD-Mark`).
    The API metadata reports `License: pd`, `Copyrighted: False`. Our check is saved in `data/filepage_check.txt`
    and `wikimedia_meta.txt`.
  - The Commons scan comes from a book (J. H. Coote, *The Illustrated History of Colour Photography*, 1993,
    per the file page). We crop it to a square (box x 250–820, y 330–900) and downsample it for qpixl-v1:
    35×35 pixels in the `aer` payloads and 6×6 pixels in the `fake_fez` and `ibm_fez` payloads (the
    `fake_brisbane` payload carries only the 112 test colours). The failed tessa-image-v1 attempts used a
    48×48 and then a 24×24 crop inside 64×64 and 32×32 plates.
- **112 test colours**: generated by `plate.py` (our own code). No third-party data.

## Engine

- **Moth Quantum Atlas, `qpixl-v1`** (Interwoven QPIXL, built on the `iqpixl` library) produced every engine
  output in this piece, 10 completed jobs in all: the `aer` simulator, the `fake_fez` noise model and the
  `fake_brisbane` noise model, each at 16, 128 and 1024 shots, plus one run on IBM Quantum hardware
  (`ibm_fez`, 1024 shots, IBM job `db1jcnuegvvc73bhn5ng`). Job IDs are in PARAMS.md.
- **Moth Quantum Atlas, `tessa-image-v1`** (the engine the brief names) was attempted 5 times. All 5 jobs failed
  with `engine_timeout` and **produced no output**. No engine output on the page or in `out/` comes from
  Tessa (`out/attempts.jsonl` only records the failures). The 5 job IDs are in PARAMS.md.

## Libraries

- three.js r128 (MIT), loaded from cdnjs, draws the 3D colour/qubit ball.
- Pillow, NumPy and requests (Python), used to prepare, extract and package the data.
- qiskit-ibm-runtime 0.45.1 with Qiskit 2.4.2 (Apache-2.0): `topology.py` reads the coupling maps of its
  fake backends (`FakeFez`, `FakeBrisbane`, `FakeTorino`) to count qubits. Already installed; nothing runs on it.
- Fonts: Geist and IBM Plex Mono (Google Fonts, SIL OFL), via the shared brand kit `common/brand.css`.
- `common/inksprite.js`, the shared pixel-sprite helper of this set (our own code).
